METHOD

Teaching Methodology

Decision-first instruction with incident simulations and architecture critique
Process
01Every session starts with a decision problem before any code is written.
02Production incident simulations train diagnosis from metrics, not guesswork.
03Architecture critique exercises expose failure modes under load and cost pressure.
04Peer code review is mandatory and graded on quality of reasoning.
05Documentation discipline includes ADRs, RFCs, traces, and incident write-ups.
ASSESSMENT

Assessment and Graduate Portfolio

Weekly deployables, peer review quality, and a public capstone portfolio
Outcomes
01Weekly deliverables require a live URL, Terraform-managed infra, tests, and CI passing.
02Weekly project work carries the largest grading weight and must be production-ready.
03Peer reviews are evaluated on depth and usefulness, not just completion.
04The capstone combines ADRs, red-team results, load tests, and a live demo.
05Every graduate leaves with public artifacts a hiring manager can inspect immediately.
